The Gut Food Bible

Indian Foods — Eat This, Not That

All from your local kirana store. No imported items. No expensive health food. This is what makes this bundle 100% practical for every Indian family.

✅

Gut-Healing Foods

  • Moong dal (sabut or dhuli)
  • Jeera + saunf water (morning)
  • Curd / homemade buttermilk (chaas)
  • Papaya (empty stomach)
  • Banana (overripe — not raw)
  • Ragi, jowar roti (instead of maida)
  • Ajwain + rock salt water
  • Bottle gourd (lauki) sabzi
  • Triphala (at night with warm water)
❌

Strict Avoidance

  • Rajma + curd in same meal
  • Cold water right after meals
  • Chai on completely empty stomach
  • Maida products (bread, biscuits daily)
  • Carbonated drinks (cold drinks)
  • Raw onion + heavy dinner combo
  • Late-night heavy meals after 9 PM
  • Excess chilli + oil in one meal
  • Eating while stressed or rushing
⏰

Daily Gut Habits

  • Lukewarm water on waking up
  • Eat every 3–4 hours (no long gaps)
  • Walk 10 min after every meal
  • Eat dinner before 8 PM strictly
  • Chew each bite 20+ times
  • No lying down right after eating
  • Warm water with jeera after dinner
  • Sleep on left side for digestion
  • Deep breathing 5 min before meals
