The Gut Food Bible

Indian Foods — Eat This, Not That

All from your local kirana store. No imported items. No expensive health food. 100% practical for every Indian family.

✅

Gut-Healing Foods

  • Moong dal (sabut or dhuli)
  • Jeera + saunf water (morning)
  • Curd / homemade chaas
  • Papaya (empty stomach)
  • Banana (overripe — not raw)
  • Ragi, jowar roti
  • Ajwain + rock salt water
  • Lauki sabzi
  • Triphala at night
❌

Strictly Avoid

  • Rajma + curd in same meal
  • Cold water right after meals
  • Chai on empty stomach
  • Maida products daily
  • Carbonated cold drinks
  • Raw onion + heavy dinner
  • Late-night heavy meals (9 PM+)
  • Excess oil + chilli in one meal
  • Eating while stressed or rushing
⏰

Daily Gut Habits

  • Lukewarm water on waking up
  • Eat every 3–4 hours (no long gaps)
  • Walk 10 min after every meal
  • Dinner before 8 PM strictly
  • Chew each bite 20+ times
  • No lying down right after eating
  • Jeera water after dinner
  • Sleep on left side for digestion
  • Deep breathing 5 min before meals
