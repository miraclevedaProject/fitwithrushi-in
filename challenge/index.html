What happens right after I pay?

You land on a confirmation page with a short form — your routine, your meal timings, anything you would rather not eat. It takes three minutes.

Basic or Premium — what actually changes?

Basic gives you the plan. You follow it, and I check in with you every two weeks.

Premium gives you the plan and me. Message me any day with any doubt — what to eat at a wedding, whether to swap something, why the scale has not moved this week. I change your diet whenever you need it, track your plate photos, and stay on it with you for the full 90 days.

Is the plan really made for me, or is it a template?

It is built from your answers — your weight, your condition, what you eat, your state, your workout, your allergies.

That is the whole reason I ask before you pay instead of after. A plan that ignores your kitchen is a plan you drop in a week.

I can't go to the gym. Will this still work?

Yes. Most women here work out at home, and plenty only walk. Tell me which one you are and your plan is built for that.

With PCOD and thyroid, the food is doing most of the work anyway.

Do I have to cook separately from my family?

No. Your plan is built from what your home already cooks — roti and sabzi, dal and rice, idli, dosa, upma, poha.

Will this work with my thyroid medicine?

Yes. Keep taking exactly what your doctor prescribed — nothing here replaces it.

Your plan is simply built around the medicine, including when you take it, so the food and the medication are not working against each other.
