No. The entire diet is built around affordable, simple, homemade Indian food. Nothing exotic, nothing that isn’t already in most Indian kitchens.

Are supplements mandatory?

No. This coaching is completely food-first. You will never be asked to buy a supplement.

I have thyroid or PCOD. Will this still work for me?

Yes — these are the exact conditions I specialise in. Mention them clearly in your application form and attach any relevant reports on WhatsApp when it opens.

I am a complete beginner. Can I join?

Absolutely. Whether you work out at the gym, at home, or just want to start with walking — the plan is built around what you can realistically do right now.

How exactly does WhatsApp support work?

You get my personal WhatsApp contact. You can message me with doubts, send meal photos for review, share progress updates and get corrections. This is not automated — it is direct, personal communication.

What happens once I submit the form?

WhatsApp opens with all your details pre-filled as a message. Send it, and I will follow up personally within 24 hours to discuss the next steps and confirm your spot.

Is the plan updated or does it stay the same throughout?

It is updated every month. Your body adapts over time and the plan evolves with it so progress never stalls.

How soon can I expect results?

Most clients see visible changes within the first 3–4 weeks. Over 3 months, the average result is 10–15 kg of fat loss when the plan is followed consistently.
