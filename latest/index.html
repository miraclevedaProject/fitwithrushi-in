No. The plan is built around simple, home-cooked Indian meals. Roti, dal, rice, sabzi, eggs, chicken – foods you already eat at home.

No calorie counting needed. You’ll get exact portion sizes in grams – simple measuring, no stress.

No. Zero supplements required. Everything you need comes from real food. I don’t sell or recommend any powders.

The plan is 100% customized. Vegetarian, non-vegetarian, Jain, allergies – everything is adjusted to fit your needs.

No. Fat loss happens 80% through diet. Walking for 30-45 minutes daily is enough. Workouts are optional but helpful.

No. You’ll eat 4-5 times a day with proper portions. No starvation. The focus is on sustainable, long-term fat loss.
Can I eat outside food or attend parties?
Yes. The plan includes smart strategies for eating out, social events, weddings, and travel – so your life doesn’t stop.

You get direct WhatsApp support, weekly progress tracking, monthly plan updates, and personal guidance for any doubts or corrections.
