Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

At Miracle Veda, we prepare and deliver a fully personalized, one-on-one fat-loss coaching program and diet plan tailored to each client's questionnaire responses, health details, and goals. Because of this personalized and time-intensive nature of our service:

Non-refundable after commencement

All payments made towards any Miracle Veda program (1-Month, 3-Month, or 6-Month plans) are non-refundable once the diet plan or coaching materials have been shared with the client, or once the program has commenced.

No refunds for change of mind or results

We do not offer refunds for change of mind, lack of results, or discontinuation of the program by the client after commencement, as outcomes depend significantly on individual adherence, consistency, and lifestyle factors outside our control.

Duplicate payments & technical errors

In the rare case of a duplicate payment or a genuine technical/payment-gateway error (e.g. amount debited twice for the same order), please contact us within 7 days with proof of payment, and we will investigate and process an appropriate resolution.

Before your program begins

If you have not yet received any plan or materials and wish to cancel before the program begins, please contact us on WhatsApp immediately — such requests will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

By completing a payment on this website, you acknowledge and agree to this Refund Policy.
