Yes. Your plan will be built around your condition, routine, and tolerances. Nothing generic – everything personalized.

Most people don’t. That’s why the plan fits your routine – not the other way around. Workout is optional – walking is enough.

You only track two things: your weekly weight + body measurements. No apps. No calorie counting. No spreadsheets.

Yes, if you want it. Day-wise workout plan with exercise names, sets, reps, and video references – for home or gym. But workout is optional.
