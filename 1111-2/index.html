How to Use This Guide

Before you start Day 1, read these 4 simple rules. They make the difference between average results and amazing results.

1

Follow the Sequence

Start from Day 1 and follow each day in order. The plan is designed to work progressively — each day builds on the previous one.

2

Do the Morning Ritual

Every morning, before anything else, follow the 3-step morning gut-reset ritual on the next page. This is non-negotiable for results.

3

Avoid Trigger Foods

The foods listed in red boxes must be completely avoided for 7 days. Even one trigger food can delay your results by 2–3 days.

4

Eat at the Right Time

Timing matters as much as what you eat. Breakfast before 9 AM, lunch between 12–2 PM, dinner before 8 PM. No eating after 9 PM.

🔬 Why Timing Matters — Science

Your gut follows a circadian rhythm — a 24-hour internal clock. Eating at irregular times disrupts gut bacteria balance, increases acid production, and slows digestion. Studies show that people who eat at consistent times have 40% less bloating than irregular eaters.

Source: Chronobiology International, 2019 · Journal of Nutritional Biochemistry
🌅 Every Morning

Morning Gut-Reset Ritual

Do this every single morning before breakfast — all 7 days. This ritual alone reduces bloating by 30–40% within the first 3 days.

⏰ Complete in 20 Minutes

6:00 AM

Wake Up + Gut-Reset Water — Immediately drink 1 glass lukewarm water (not cold, not hot — just warm). Add ½ tsp jeera (cumin) powder or steep overnight jeera water. Drink slowly on empty stomach. This activates your digestive system and flushes overnight toxins.

6:10 AM

10-Minute Walk or Light Movement — Walk around your home, do light stretching, or 10 surya namaskars. Movement stimulates gut motility — the muscle contractions that push food through your intestines. This is the #1 natural constipation and gas reliever.

6:20 AM

Triphala (Optional but Powerful) — If you have triphala powder, take ½ tsp with warm water. This Ayurvedic formula cleanses the colon, improves bowel movement, and reduces acidity. Skip if you’re pregnant or on medication — consult doctor first.

7:00 AM

Breakfast — After 40 minutes minimum from gut-reset water, have your Day’s breakfast as listed in the plan below. Never skip breakfast — it sets your gut’s digestive tone for the entire day.

🌿 Ayurveda Says — Dinacharya (Daily Routine)

Ayurveda’s concept of Dinacharya teaches that waking up before sunrise and drinking warm water activates Agni (digestive fire). Modern science confirms this — morning warm water increases metabolism by 24% and stimulates bowel movement within 30 minutes in most people.

🔬 The Overeating-Bloating Connection

When you overeat even healthy foods, your stomach stretches beyond capacity. This triggers excess gas production as gut bacteria work overtime to ferment the undigested food. Eating to 80% fullness (called Hara Hachi Bu in Japanese longevity science) reduces bloating by up to 50%.

Source: American Journal of Gastroenterology · Ayurvedic text Charaka Samhita
