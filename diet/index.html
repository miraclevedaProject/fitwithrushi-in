🥗 SALAD GUIDELINES

Salads should be simple, fresh, and lightly seasoned. The purpose is fiber, minerals, inflammation control, and gut support — not heavy calories.

✅ ALLOWED SALAD VEGETABLES

  • Cucumber
  • Tomato
  • Carrot (grated or sliced)
  • Beetroot (boiled or lightly steamed)
  • Onion (small quantity)
  • Capsicum
  • Lettuce
  • Cabbage (finely chopped)
  • Radish
  • Sprouts (lightly steamed, optional)
  • Coriander leaves
  • Curry leaves
  • Mint leaves

➕ OPTIONAL PROTEIN ADD-ONS

Use only when advised in the meal plan:

  • Paneer cubes (50–80 g)
  • Boiled egg (1 whole or 2 whites)
  • Sprouts (50–100 g)
  • Tofu (50–80 g)
  • Grilled chicken (50–80 g)

🫒 DRESSING RULES (VERY IMPORTANT)

  • Lemon juice
  • A pinch of rock salt
  • Black pepper
  • Roasted cumin powder
  • No mayonnaise
  • No ready-made dressings
  • No vinegar
  • No cheese
  • No sauces or chutneys

Oil is NOT required in salads.

📏 PORTION SIZE

  • Vegetable salad: 1 medium bowl (120–150 g)
  • Protein salad: As per quantity mentioned in the meal plan

⏰ BEST TIME TO CONSUME SALAD: Before lunch, with lunch, or early evening (before 7 PM). Avoid raw salads late at night to prevent bloating.

🚫 AVOID IN SALADS

  • Raw cauliflower (at night)
  • Excess onion
  • Corn
  • Boiled potato
  • Peanuts (unless mentioned)
  • Packaged croutons
  • Sweet dressings

📌 IMPORTANT NOTE: Salads support digestion and fat loss only when kept light and simple. Heavy salads with sauces can increase inflammation and slow results.

🔔 IMPORTANT INSTRUCTIONS & GUIDELINES

Please read and follow these instructions carefully to get the best possible results from your diet plan.

💧 Hydration

  • Maintain 3–4 litres of water intake daily, consumed in small, frequent sips throughout the day
  • Avoid ice-cold water, especially during or immediately after meals

🍽️ Eating Habits

  • Eat slowly and chew food properly to support digestion and reduce bloating
  • Do not skip meals under any circumstances
  • Follow portion control strictly; overeating, even healthy food, can delay fat loss

🥗 Food Quality & Variety

  • Include a wide variety of foods from the diet plan to ensure adequate intake of essential nutrients
  • Consume salads whenever mentioned in the meal plan
  • Avoid repetitive food choices unless specifically advised

🫒 Cooking Guidelines

  • Use 1 teaspoon (5 ml) oil per meal only for cooking
  • Prefer home-cooked food prepared using recommended oils
  • Consume roti without ghee

🫖 Beverages

  • Tea or coffee is allowed once per day (150 ml) OR ½ cup twice a day
  • Always prepare with milk and without sugar
  • Avoid multiple cups or late-evening consumption

🌿 Anti-Inflammatory Support

  • You may include 2 teaspoons of roasted flaxseeds daily (optional but recommended)
  • Ensure regular intake of vegetables and anti-inflammatory foods mentioned in the plan

☀️ Sunlight & Recovery

  • Take a minimum of 20 minutes of sunlight exposure daily for vitamin D support and overall hormonal health
  • Ensure adequate sleep and recovery to support fat loss

🚶‍♀️ Physical Activity

  • Engage in regular physical activity such as walking and yoga, as advised
  • Avoid high-impact or extreme workouts unless cleared by a professional

📌 Consistency Rule: This plan works best when followed consistently. Irregular eating, skipping meals, or frequent deviations can reduce results.
