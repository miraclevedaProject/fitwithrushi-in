The 3P System™ — What Makes This Different

Three interconnected pillars that convert protein confusion into permanent competence. Not a diet. A framework you use for life.

Pillar 1

Price Intelligence

One metric — cost per 10g protein — eliminates all grocery confusion permanently. Know which foods deliver maximum protein per rupee, automatically.

Example: Soya chunks = 10g protein for ₹4.23. Whey protein = ₹25.00 for the same. Same protein, 6× the cost.
Pillar 2

Protein Planning

Strategic distribution across meals. When you eat matters as much as what you eat. Optimal 20–25g spread across 3–4 meals maximizes absorption.

Example: Same 90g protein spread correctly builds muscle far more effectively than loading 70g in one meal.
Pillar 3

Protein Pairing

Amino acid complementation science — the reason dal + rice has been India’s nutritional backbone for thousands of years. Combine correctly, maximize quality.

Example: Dal + rice = complete amino profile at ₹15 — more complete than most ₹100 meals when paired correctly.
