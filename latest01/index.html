Common Questions & Fat-Loss Myths

Everything you need to know before starting

1. Will I have to stop eating my normal Indian food?
No. You’ll eat the same food you already cook at home – just structured properly. No fancy ingredients, no imported nonsense.
2. Do I need to count calories or track macros?
No. You’ll only measure portions. Calorie counting is overkill for normal people with real jobs.
3. Can this work if I have PCOS, Thyroid, or insulin resistance?
Yes. Your plan will be built around your condition, routine, and tolerances. Nothing generic – everything personalized.
4. I don’t have time for meal prep or gym.
Most people don’t. That’s why the plan fits your routine – not the other way around. Even busy people lose fat when the structure is right. Workout is optional – walking is enough.
5. I lose weight… then gain it all back.
Because your old plans were not sustainable. When a diet fits your real life, results don’t bounce back.
6. I’ll have to give up my favourite foods.
No. You adjust portions – you don’t eliminate foods. This program feels like life, not punishment.
7. I can’t afford supplements or fancy food.
Good – you don’t need them. Normal Indian home-cooked food is all you’ll use. No whey, no detox drinks, no imported items.
8. There’s too much conflicting advice online.
Correct. 90% of it is noise. Here you get one clear system – no confusion, no guessing.
9. Tracking is too complicated.
You only track two things: your weekly weight + body measurements. No apps. No calorie counting. No spreadsheets.
10. Will I get a workout plan?
Yes, if you want it. You’ll get a day-wise workout plan with exercise names, sets, reps, and video references – whether you prefer home workouts or the gym. But workout is optional – diet is the main focus.
11. How fast will I see results?
Most clients report visible changes within 7–14 days. Weight loss varies based on your starting point, but the system is designed for steady, sustainable progress.
12. Maybe weight loss just doesn’t work for me.
It works – just not with the wrong approach. When the plan fits your routine, your numbers start moving. Consistency becomes easy, not exhausting.
